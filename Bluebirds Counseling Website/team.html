Founder & Clinical Director

Leila Kandahari

M.S. Psy., MSW · Licensed in VA & MD leila.kandahari@bluebirdscounseling.com (703) 232-0033

Leila is a Licensed Clinical Social Worker (LCSW) in Virginia and Maryland, and the founder and clinical director of Bluebirds Counseling and Recovery Solutions. She holds a B.S. and M.S. in Clinical Psychology and a second master’s degree in Advanced Clinical Social Work, with over a decade of experience specializing in substance abuse and trauma services — including co-occurring disorders, MAT programs, and work with service members and their families. As a culturally and linguistically competent professional, she is passionate about raising mental health awareness within minority communities.

Therapist

Zhila Qandahari

M.S. in Marriage and Family Counseling zhila.q@bluebirdscounseling.com

Zhila is a dedicated mental health counselor with over ten years of clinical experience supporting individuals facing mood disorders, trauma, life transitions, and complex mental health needs — including extensive work with young adults, adults, and refugee populations across individual, couples, and group therapy. She holds a Master’s degree in Marriage and Family Counseling and certified trainings in CBT, REBT, EFT, Hypnotism, and Neurocognitive Interventions in Eating Disorders. Fluent in Dari and Persian, Zhila offers bilingual, culturally sensitive care focused on helping clients regain autonomy.

Therapist

Ammal Mahmood

M.Ed. in Clinical Mental Health Counseling ammal.m@bluebirdscounseling.com

Ammal Mahmood is a first-generation college graduate and Resident in Counseling who strives to create a warm, compassionate, and culturally responsive space where clients feel safe to be themselves. She works with individuals, couples, and families navigating anxiety, cultural identity, life transitions, and relationship dynamics, drawing from CBT, Solution-Focused Therapy, Person-Centered Therapy, Family Systems Therapy, Acceptance and Commitment Therapy, and Multicultural Counseling. She holds an M.Ed. in Clinical Mental Health Counseling and a B.S. in Psychology, and provides counseling in English, Urdu, Hindi, and Punjabi.
